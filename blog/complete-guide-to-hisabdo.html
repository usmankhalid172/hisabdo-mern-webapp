Complete Guide to HisabDo: Features and How to Use It

Everything you need to know about HisabDo — from getting started to advanced features — in one comprehensive guide for new and existing users.

By Mian Usman Khalid  •  Published: May 10, 2026  •  15 min read

HisabDo is an offline-first khata and ledger app designed for shopkeepers, freelancers, small business owners and anyone who needs a simple, reliable way to manage financial records. This guide covers everything you need to know to get the most out of the app — from initial setup to advanced features.

What Is HisabDo?

HisabDo is a mobile application available on Android that helps you manage your daily financial records. The name combines "Hisab" (accounting/calculation in Urdu) with "Do" (do/perform), reflecting the app's purpose: making financial management something you can actually do, easily and consistently.

The app is built around an offline-first philosophy: your data is stored on your device, not on remote servers. This means HisabDo works without an internet connection, your financial records remain private and the app is fast and responsive regardless of your connectivity situation.

Who Is HisabDo For?

HisabDo is designed for a wide range of users:

  • Shopkeepers: Track customer credit, manage daily sales and monitor outstanding balances
  • Freelancers: Record client payments, track business expenses and manage invoices
  • Small business owners: Manage receivables, payables, expenses and generate reports
  • Students: Track personal expenses and manage a simple budget
  • Home-based sellers: Keep records of orders, payments and customer balances
  • Service providers: Track service fees, customer accounts and payment history

Core Features

Khata and Ledger Management

The core of HisabDo is its khata management system. You can record every financial transaction — money given, money received, expenses and income — with a date, amount, description and customer association. The app maintains a running balance for each account, so you always know your current position.

Customer Management

Create individual profiles for each customer with their name, contact information and any relevant notes. Each customer has their own transaction history and running balance. You can search for any customer instantly and see their complete financial history at a glance.

Receivables and Payables

HisabDo tracks both what customers owe you (receivables) and what you owe to others (payables). This dual tracking gives you a complete picture of your financial obligations and helps you manage cash flow effectively.

PDF Export

Generate professional PDF reports for any customer account or time period. These reports can be shared via WhatsApp, email or any other messaging platform directly from your phone. PDF export is useful for customer statements, accountant records and personal backups.

Analytics

HisabDo's analytics features provide visual summaries of your financial activity — income trends, expense patterns, outstanding balances and more. These insights help you understand your business's financial health without manual calculation.

Voice Entry

Record transactions by speaking rather than typing. Voice entry is particularly useful in busy environments where typing is slow or disruptive. Simply speak the transaction details and HisabDo creates the entry automatically.

Backup and Restore

Protect your financial records with HisabDo's backup feature. Create a backup of all your data and store it on your device, an SD card or a cloud storage service of your choice. If you change phones or experience a device problem, restore your data from the backup to continue exactly where you left off.

Multi-Language Support

HisabDo supports Urdu, English, Hindi, Arabic and Roman Urdu. Switch between languages in the app settings to use the interface in your preferred language.

Multi-Currency Support

The app supports Pakistani Rupee (PKR), US Dollar (USD) and Indian Rupee (INR), making it suitable for users across Pakistan, India and the South Asian diaspora.

Getting Started

Getting started with HisabDo takes less than five minutes:

  • Download HisabDo from Google Play
  • Open the app and select your preferred language and currency
  • Add your first customer by entering their name and contact details
  • Record your first transaction — either money given or money received
  • View the customer's balance and transaction history

Best Practices for HisabDo Users

  • Record every transaction the same day it happens
  • Create a separate customer profile for each person or business you transact with
  • Use descriptive notes for each entry to make records easier to review later
  • Back up your data weekly
  • Generate monthly PDF summaries for your records
  • Review outstanding balances weekly and follow up on overdue accounts

Why HisabDo Was Built

HisabDo was created by Mian Usman Khalid, a Pakistani software engineer who recognized the gap between the financial tools available to large businesses and the practical needs of small shopkeepers and entrepreneurs. The app was designed to bring the benefits of digital financial management to users who may not have accounting knowledge, reliable internet access or the budget for expensive software.

The result is an app that is powerful enough for serious business use but simple enough for anyone to use from day one — offline, private and free.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is HisabDo free to use?

Yes, HisabDo is free to download and use for core functionality. It is available on Google Play for Android devices.

Does HisabDo work without internet?

Yes. Core features — recording transactions, viewing balances, managing customer accounts and generating reports — all work without an internet connection. Optional cloud features are available for signed-in users. See the Privacy Policy for details on data storage.

What languages does HisabDo support?

HisabDo supports Urdu, English, Hindi, Arabic and Roman Urdu. You can switch between languages in the app settings.

How do I back up my HisabDo data?

HisabDo includes a backup feature that allows you to create a backup of all your data. You can store the backup on your device, an SD card or a cloud storage service of your choice. To restore, use the restore feature and select your backup file.
