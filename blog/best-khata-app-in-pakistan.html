What to Look for in a Khata App for Pakistan

Key features that matter when choosing a digital khata app for your shop, business or household — and why each one makes a practical difference.

By Mian Usman Khalid  •  Published: April 18, 2026  •  10 min read

The khata — the daily record of money given and received — is central to how millions of businesses operate across Pakistan. From the corner grocery store to the wholesale market, from the tailor to the mobile repair shop, the khata register is how business owners track what customers owe and what they have paid.

As smartphones have become ubiquitous, digital khata apps have emerged as a natural evolution of this tradition. But with multiple options available, how do you choose the right one? This guide covers the key features to look for and explains why each one matters for Pakistani business owners.

1. Offline-First Operation

This is the most important feature for any khata app used in Pakistan. Internet connectivity is improving but remains unreliable in many areas — rural markets, basement shops, areas with poor signal and situations where data is expensive or unavailable. A khata app that requires an internet connection to function is not suitable for real-world Pakistani business conditions.

Look for an app that stores all data locally on your device and works fully without any internet connection. HisabDo is built on this principle — your records are always accessible, regardless of connectivity.

2. Urdu Language Support

For many Pakistani business owners, Urdu is the primary language of business communication. A khata app that only supports English creates a barrier for users who are more comfortable in Urdu. Look for apps that support Urdu script, Roman Urdu or both, along with English for users who prefer it.

HisabDo supports Urdu, English, Hindi, Arabic and Roman Urdu, making it accessible to a wide range of users across Pakistan and the broader South Asian region.

3. Customer Management

A good khata app should allow you to create individual profiles for each customer, track their complete transaction history and see their current balance at a glance. This is the core functionality that makes a digital khata more useful than a paper register.

The ability to search for a specific customer instantly — rather than flipping through pages — is one of the most practical advantages of digital khata management.

4. PDF Export

The ability to generate a professional PDF statement for a customer or time period is increasingly important for Pakistani businesses. Whether you need to share a statement with a customer who disputes a balance, provide records to an accountant or simply keep a printed backup, PDF export is a feature that adds real value.

5. PKR Currency Support

This seems obvious, but not all financial apps are designed with Pakistani Rupee (PKR) as a primary currency. Look for an app that supports PKR natively, with proper formatting and display. HisabDo supports PKR, USD and INR, covering the currencies most commonly used by Pakistani businesses and the diaspora.

6. Backup and Restore

Your khata records represent months or years of business history. Losing them to a broken phone or accidental deletion is a serious problem. Look for an app with reliable backup features that allow you to restore your data to a new device if needed.

7. Simple, Intuitive Interface

A khata app is only useful if you actually use it consistently. An interface that is complicated, slow or confusing will be abandoned within days. Look for an app that mirrors the familiar flow of paper khata management — add a customer, record a transaction, check the balance — but with the speed and reliability of a digital system.

8. Privacy and Data Security

Your khata records contain sensitive information about your customers, your business relationships and your financial position. Look for an app that keeps this data on your device rather than uploading it to external servers, and that does not share your financial records with third parties for advertising or other purposes.

9. Voice Entry

In a busy shop environment, typing entries can be slow and disruptive. Voice entry — the ability to create a record by speaking — significantly reduces the friction of recording transactions and makes it more likely that you will record every entry consistently.

10. Free to Use

Many Pakistani small businesses operate on tight margins. A khata app that charges a monthly subscription fee creates an ongoing cost that may not be justified for smaller operations. Look for an app that is free to download and use for core functionality.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most important feature in a khata app for Pakistan?

Offline operation is the most critical feature for Pakistani users. Internet connectivity is unreliable in many areas, and a khata app that stops working without internet is not suitable for real-world business conditions. All core features — recording transactions, viewing balances, generating reports — should work without any internet connection.

Does a khata app need to support Urdu?

For many Pakistani business owners, Urdu language support significantly improves usability. An app that only supports English creates a barrier for users who are more comfortable in Urdu. Look for apps that support Urdu script, Roman Urdu or both, in addition to English.

How do I back up my khata records if I change phones?
