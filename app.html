What is HisabDo?

HisabDo is an advanced offline digital ledger designed to manage khata, udhar, receivables, payables, customers, and business expenses in one simple system.

Built for shopkeepers, freelancers and small businesses, HisabDo replaces manual registers with fast digital bookkeeping. It keeps your records organized, your balances accurate, and your reports ready to share.
