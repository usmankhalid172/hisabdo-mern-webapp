Real Estate Entrepreneur

Mian Ahsan Khalid is a Pakistani entrepreneur and real estate professional associated with Realtek Developers. He is involved in property advisory, investment consulting, and business operations within the real estate industry.

As Co-Founder of HisabDo, Ahsan plays a key role in shaping business strategy, partnerships, and customer outreach. He brings practical experience from managing real estate projects and advising clients on financial planning — skills that translate into stronger product-market fit and real-world product decisions.

His approach focuses on measurable outcomes: improving customer retention, simplifying onboarding, and building durable partnerships that support local businesses. Ahsan helps ensure that product features align with the needs of shopkeepers, freelancers and small enterprises.
