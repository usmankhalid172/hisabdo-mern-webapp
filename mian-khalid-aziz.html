Decades of Business Leadership & Entrepreneurship

Mian Khalid Aziz is a Pakistani businessman and entrepreneur with decades of experience in business, real estate, construction, and investment. He has played an important role in shaping the entrepreneurial values of the family and serves as a mentor to the HisabDo leadership team.

As Co-Founder of HisabDo, he provides strategic guidance, business insight, and long-term vision, drawing upon his extensive experience in entrepreneurship and investment.

His decades of experience inform product-level decisions such as market fit, pricing, and long-term partnerships. He also mentors the founding team on operational discipline and risk management.
