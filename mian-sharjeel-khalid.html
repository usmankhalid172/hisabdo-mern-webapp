About

Entrepreneur & Real Estate Professional

Mian Sharjeel Khalid is a Pakistani entrepreneur, real estate professional, and property advisor based in Bahria Town Lahore. He serves as the Sales Director at Realtek Developers and is actively involved in residential and commercial real estate, investment consulting, property development, and construction.

As Co-Founder of HisabDo, he contributes business perspective and product feedback on direction and growth decisions, bringing practical, real-world business experience from the real estate industry. The app's technical development is led by Founder & CEO Mian Usman Khalid.

Sharjeel focuses on customer workflows, merchant onboarding, and articulating real user problems to the engineering team. His input helps prioritize features that increase revenue reliability and reduce friction for sellers.
