1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use the App.

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Name (if provided)
  • Email address
  • Authentication credentials (managed securely by Supabase Authentication)

We do not store your password.

Business & Financial Data

When you use the App, you may create and store:

  • Customer names, phone numbers, and email addresses
  • Notes, income records, expense records, transaction amounts
  • Categories, dates, images attached to transactions
  • Business profile information

This information is stored locally on your device and, if you are signed in, is synchronized securely with our cloud database.

Device Information

The App may automatically collect limited technical information, including:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash logs (if applicable)

This information is used only for improving app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate your identity
  • Sync your data across your devices
  • Backup your business records
  • Restore your information after reinstalling the app
  • Improve app reliability
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain security

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Cloud Storage & Synchronization

If you sign in to the App:

  • Your business data is securely synchronized with our cloud servers.
  • Synchronization allows access to your data across multiple devices.
  • Data transfers are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.

Cloud synchronization is optional and requires signing into your account.

4. Local Storage

The App stores data locally on your device to provide fast offline access. Local data may include:

  • Customers, income, expenses
  • Settings, categories, business profile

The App continues to work offline. Changes made while offline are synchronized automatically when an internet connection becomes available.

5. Authentication

User authentication is securely managed using Supabase Authentication. Authentication may include email and password login. Passwords are never stored by the application itself.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures including:

  • HTTPS encrypted communication
  • Secure authentication
  • Access control and database security rules

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Your cloud data remains associated with your account until you delete your account or request deletion of your data.

Local data stored on your device may be removed if you uninstall the App, clear app storage, or sign out (depending on app behavior).

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your information
  • Update or correct your information

To request deletion, contact us using the details below.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

12. Internet Requirement

Some features require an internet connection, including:

  • Login and registration
  • Cloud synchronization
  • Account recovery

Core business features continue to work offline. Data created offline will synchronize after you reconnect and sign in.

13. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data:

  • When required by law
  • To protect our legal rights
  • With trusted service providers (such as Supabase) necessary to operate the App
